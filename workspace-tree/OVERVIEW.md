Adds a "Workspace tree" sidebar view that shows each project's workspaces as a collapsible tree. A workspace started from another workspace, for example when an agent spins up separate workspaces for several bugs, appears nested under the one that started it. You can see at a glance which workspaces belong together.

Select it in Settings > Sidebar > Sidebar view. Click a workspace to open it. Click the arrow beside a parent or project to fold it; a folded parent shows how many workspaces it hides. Folded state is saved on this device only. The filter button on the right of the first project's header opens a list of hosts and projects; ticking some limits the tree to them, nothing ticked shows everything, and the choice is also saved on this device only.

Under the tree, a Reviews panel lists open pull requests that request your review, plus ones you approved that have new commits since or whose review was dismissed. Each row shows the same CI, merge conflict and review badges as the pull requests under your workspaces, and opens the pull request in your browser. Requests are sorted by repository. The ⋯ button beside refresh opens a small menu (a sheet on phones) that chooses which repositories are listed and whether drafts and re-approvals are; what it leaves out is not counted, and the choice applies on every device. It reads GitHub through the `gh` CLI on the daemon machine, so `gh` must be installed and logged in there. Below it, three bars show how much of its context window the agent you are viewing has used, and your Claude plan's 5-hour and weekly usage; press them for every usage window, its reset time, and when it runs out at your current rate.

The dot beside each workspace follows Paseo's status: running, needs input, finished, or failed. A child whose parent has been archived moves to the top level. Workspaces are ordered newest first.
